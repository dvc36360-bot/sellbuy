🚀 Get Started

The fastest route from joining the server to using your first bending ability.

Join play.bendersmc.co. Bedrock players use port 19132.
Use /spawn or /choose to open the bending selection.
Pick your element and choose the staff loadout or manually bind abilities.
Use /b bind ability slot to put moves on your hotbar.
Use /b help ability when you need the controls for a specific move.

🎮 Java & Bedrock

BendersMC advertises cross-platform Java + Bedrock play. Bedrock connection uses the same server address with port 19132.

🌪️ Air — documented controls

AirSpoutLeft click creates a stream below you. Shift/crouch moves down; jump moves up. Click again to deactivate.
TornadoCreates a tornado. Shift/crouch while looking where you want it.
FlatulateLaunches the user into the air. Shift/crouch to activate.
ZephyrHold shift/crouch to form an air-particle circle that applies slow falling.
Air BlastClick pushes enemies away. Shift/crouch creates an air orb that drags what you click toward it.
Air SuctionClick pulls enemies toward you. Shift/crouch creates an air orb that pulls targets toward it.
Air GlideWhile airborne, shift/crouch to glide; shift/crouch again or land to stop.
Air Scooter • Meditate • Air Lift • Air JetsNames listed by the official guide; use /b help <ability> in-game for the current instructions.

🌎 Survival Guide

/rtp

After choosing an element, BendersMC's Survival guide says you can use /rtp to enter the world. You can also use the “Enter World” NPC at spawn.

Land claims

Get a golden shovel, mark one corner with right-click, then mark the other corner with right-click. Claim blocks are earned through playtime.

Player warps

Survival includes player warps that can be used for stores, public farms and other community locations.

Custom gameplay

BendersMC describes Survival as vanilla-style gameplay with bending, custom items, multiple worlds and a large world to explore.
